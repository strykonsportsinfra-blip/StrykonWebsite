      Questions

      Before you build

      Can't find your answer? Send us your plot size and a few photos on WhatsApp and we'll talk it through.

      How much does a project cost?

      It depends on the area, the condition of the site, the materials and the scope. After a site visit we give you a fixed, itemised quote, so you can see exactly where the money goes. A plot size and a few photos are enough for a first estimate.

      How do I book a site visit?

      Use the booking form on this page to pick a date and time, or send us a WhatsApp message. The visit is added to our calendar and our team calls you to confirm.

      Do you take on projects beyond sports facilities?

      Yes. Our infrastructure division builds independent houses and apartments, commercial and institutional buildings, civil works such as roads, compound walls and drainage, solar power plants and Indian railway projects.

      How long does a build take?

      Most single courts take a few weeks once site work starts. Buildings, full-size grounds and projects with major civil works take longer, and new concrete bases need curing time. Your quote includes a dated schedule.

      Can you convert an old cement court?

      Usually, yes. If the base is sound, we repair cracks, correct the levels and lay a new acrylic or tile surface over it. If the base has failed, we show you why and quote for a new one.

      Can one area be used for more than one sport?

      Yes. Basketball courts can carry volleyball and throwball lines, and tennis courts can carry pickleball lines, each marked in its own colour. Multi-sport turf can host football and cricket practice.

      What maintenance does a surface or solar plant need?

      Artificial turf needs regular brushing and occasional infill top-ups, acrylic courts need washing and a recoat every few years, and solar panels need periodic cleaning and an annual check. We offer scheduled maintenance and support visits.
